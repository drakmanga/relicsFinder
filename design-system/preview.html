02

Elevation

No box-shadow: clip-path would cut it away. Depth is surface + border intensity. Only the floating layers (e3, e4) may add drop-shadow.

03

Orokin geometry

border-radius: 0 everywhere except dots and spinners. The silhouette is clip-path. Clip containers only — never tr, td, li, avatars.

Le tre forme

clip-orokin

Default. Cuts top-left and bottom-right.

clip-orokin-inverse

The mirrored half of the layout. Detail panel.

clip-octagon

Chips and badges only.

Frames — the double-surface technique

.o-frame

1px border-default. The wrapper is the border.

.o-frame--interactive

#8A7423 border — 3:1 verified, mandatory on controls.

.o-frame--gilded

Orokin inlay: a 135° gradient, gold on the two cuts.

The inner notch is notch − 1px: without the subtraction the border along the diagonal comes out about 1.41× thinner.

11

Do / Don't

Le regole che una code review deve far rispettare.

✅ clip-path on panels, cards, buttons, chips
❌ clip-path on tr, td, li, avatars
✅ Borders through the double-surface .o-frame technique
❌ border or outline directly on a clipped element
✅ Elevation from surface + border
❌ box-shadow — the clip cuts it away
✅ Gold for Rare, Radiant, brand, primary action
❌ Gold for links — links are void-400
✅ void-700 as a fill with text
❌ void-500 as a fill with text — 3.97:1
✅ Rarity = dot + text
❌ Rarity = colour alone
✅ tabular-nums on every numeric column
❌ Proportional digits in prices
✅ Cinzel ≥18px, never uppercase on top
❌ Cinzel at 13px inside a table cell
✅ One primary per view
❌ Three gold buttons side by side
✅ One pulsing Radiant element per view
❌ Thirty rows pulsing together
✅ — for a missing price
❌ 0 p for a missing price
✅ Below lg: table → cards
❌ Horizontal table scrolling on mobile
